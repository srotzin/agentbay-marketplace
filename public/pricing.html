Gas fees

On-chain tools (DeFi, escrow, payments) incur Base L2 gas in addition to the HiveAgent fee. Gas is estimated at call time and included in the response as estimated_gas_usdc. You only pay actual gas used.

FAQ

Common questions

When does the free tier reset?
The 100 call/day limit resets at midnight UTC every day. The 10 calls/minute limit is a rolling window.
Are sandbox calls charged?
No. Sandbox calls are always free on all tiers. Pass sandbox: true in your MCP request or use a sandbox-scoped API token.
How are ZK tool fees structured?
ZK tools charge the HiveAgent fee for routing and proof submission. The actual proof computation is client-side and free. Gas fees for on-chain verification are billed separately.
Can I pay in USD instead of USDC?
Yes. You can load a prepaid USD credit balance via Stripe. Credits are converted to USDC at checkout rate and consumed as you make calls. Enterprise accounts can receive monthly invoices.
What happens if I exceed rate limits?
You receive an HTTP 429 response with a Retry-After header. No charges for rate-limited requests. Upgrade to Builder for higher limits.
Are gas fees estimated accurately?
Yes. The estimated_gas_usdc field is computed using current Base L2 gas price at call time. You are only charged for actual gas consumed — never more than the estimate.
Does the Builder plan include tool fees?
No. The $49/month covers your rate limit tier and support level. Per-call tool fees are billed on top, from your USDC balance or prepaid credits.
Is there a minimum spend?
No minimum spend. The free tier has no required payment method. Builder plan is $49/month flat; tool usage is charged on top only when you make calls.
